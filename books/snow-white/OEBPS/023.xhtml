Shortly after the Dwarfs left, the old peddler woman
 appeared at the cottage. “All alone, my pet?” she asked. Snow
 White nodded as the old woman sniffed the air. “Makin’ pies?”

“Yes,” said the princess, “gooseberry pies.”

“Ahh, it’s apple pies that make the menfolks’ mouths water,”
 the old woman told her. “Pies made with apples like these.”
 She lifted a shiny red apple from her basket. “Like to try one,
 dearie? Go on, have a bite.”
