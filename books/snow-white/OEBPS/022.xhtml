Back at the cottage, Snow White was saying good-bye to
 the Dwarfs as they set off for work. As she kissed each one
 on the head, Doc stood close by. “Now don’t forget, my
 dear, the old Queen’s a sly one,” he reminded her. “Full of
 witchcraft, so beware.”

Then Grumpy frowned. “Now, I’m warnin’ ya,” he said,
 “don’t let nobody or nothin’ in the house.”

“Why, Grumpy, you do care!” exclaimed Snow White.
