A sudden storm began to rage
 as the Dwarfs reached the cottage,
 where they found the lifeless Snow
 White. Through the rain, Grumpy
 spotted the old hag disappearing
 into the forest.

The Dwarfs chased the Queen
 up a steep cliff. “You little fools,” she
 said. “I’ll crush yer bones!”

She tried desperately to pry
 a boulder loose to crush them.
 Suddenly, a bolt of lightning
 shattered the ledge, sending the
 wicked Queen into the valley below.
