Doc slowly opened the door and
 peered in. “Why, i-it-it’s a girl,” he said.

As the Dwarfs approached the
 sleeping princess, she began to stir. “She’s
 wakin’ up! Hide!” they cried.

Doc dashed behind the beds, and the
 other Dwarfs ran after him.

Snow White yawned and stretched.
 Then she noticed seven pairs of eyes
 looking at her over the ends of the beds.
 She sat up, smiling, and asked, “How do
 you do?”

“How do you do what?” said Grumpy
 as he folded his arms, scowling.

“Let me guess. You must be Grumpy,”
 Snow White said with a laugh.

“I know who I am. Who are you?”
 replied the dwarf.

“Oh, how silly of me,” she said. “I’m
 Snow White.”

“The princess?” Doc asked. He looked
 very impressed.
