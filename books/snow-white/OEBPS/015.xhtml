Grumpy, true to his name, crossed his arms and glared. “Mark my
 words,” he said, “there’s trouble a-brewin’. I felt it comin’ on all day.”

Suddenly, the Dwarfs thought they heard a sound. Doc looked
 toward the stairs. “I-it-it’s up there. In the bedroom,” he whispered.

Cautiously, the seven little men went to investigate.
