Magic mirror on the wall . . .

Snow White, a kind and beautiful
princess, charms every creature in
the kingdom except one—the jealous
Queen. When Snow White is forced
to flee the kingdom, she befriends
seven loveable dwarfs—Doc, Sneezy,
Grumpy, Happy, Bashful, Sleepy, and
Dopey. But when the Queen finds and
tricks Snow White with an enchanted
apple, only the magic of Love’s First
Kiss can save her!
