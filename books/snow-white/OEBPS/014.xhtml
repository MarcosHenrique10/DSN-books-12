Just then, the owners of the cottage came marching home.
 They weren’t children at all, but seven dwarfs who worked all
 day in their diamond mine.

As they came into the clearing, the one named Doc made
 everyone halt. He peered through his glasses. “Look—our
 house!” he cried. “The lit’s light! The light’s lit! Door’s open.
 Chimney’s smokin’. Somethin’s in there!”

The Dwarfs peeked inside the cottage. “Why, why the
 whole place is clean!” said Doc.
