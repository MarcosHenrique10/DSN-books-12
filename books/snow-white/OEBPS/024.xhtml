Sensing that Snow White was in danger, several birds
 swooped down on the woman, knocking the apple out of her
 hand. Snow White tried to shoo the birds away. “Stop it!” she
 cried. “Go away! Shame on you, frightening the poor old lady.”

“Oh, my heart! Oh, my, my poor heart,” said the old
 woman. “Take me into the house and let me rest. A drink of
 water, please.”

Unable to make Snow White understand, the birds and
 animals raced to alert the Dwarfs.
