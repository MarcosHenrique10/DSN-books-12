She knocked on the door, but no one answered. So she went inside.

“Oh, it’s adorable!” Snow White exclaimed. “Just like a doll’s house.
 Why, there’s seven little chairs! There must be seven little children.”
 The princess glanced around. “And by the looks of this table, seven
 untidy children.”

This gave Snow White an idea. “I know,” she said, “I’ll clean house
 and surprise them; then maybe they’ll let me stay.”
