One day, as Snow White was drawing water from a well,
 she made a wish. She wished that the one she loved would
 find her, and she dreamed about how nice he would be.

As she gazed into the wishing well, she saw another face
 reflected in the water. It belonged to a handsome prince.
 “Hello,” said the Prince. “Did I frighten you? Please don’t
 run away!”

But the startled princess had fled to her balcony, where
 she could watch him from afar.
