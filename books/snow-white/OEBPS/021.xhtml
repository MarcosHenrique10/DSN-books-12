Back in the castle, the wicked Queen stood before her mirror. “Magic
 Mirror on the wall, who now is the fairest one of all?” she asked.

“Beyond the seventh fall, in the cottage of the Seven Dwarfs,” the
 mirror told her, “Snow White still lives, fairest one of all.”

“I’ve been tricked!” she cried. “I’ll go myself to the Dwarfs’ cottage in
 the woods. I’ll go in a disguise so complete no one will suspect me.”

The Queen concocted a magic potion, then transformed herself into
 an ugly old peddler woman. “And now,” she began, “a special sort of
 death for one so fair. Ah, a poisoned apple!” As soon as Snow White
 tasted the poisoned apple, her eyes would close forever, only to be
 opened by Love’s First Kiss.
