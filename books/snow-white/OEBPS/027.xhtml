The old woman grinned at the princess. “Perhaps there’s
 someone you love?” she asked.

“Well, there is someone,” said Snow White, remembering
 her prince.

“I thought so,” said the woman. “Old Granny knows a
 young girl’s heart. Now make a wish and take a bite.”

Snow White did so. “Oh, I feel strange,” she said.

A moment later, she fell to the ground.
