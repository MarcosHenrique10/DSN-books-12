But Grumpy frowned.
 “Tell her to go back where she
 belongs,” he said.

“Please don’t send me away!”
 she pleaded. “If you do, the
 Queen will kill me!”

Grumpy shook his head.
 “The Queen’s an old witch,”
 he said. “If she finds you here,
 she’ll swoop down and wreak her
 vengeance on all of us!”

“Oh, she’ll never find me
 here,” promised Snow White.
 “And if you let me stay, I’ll keep
 house for you. I’ll wash and sew
 and sweep and cook. . . .”

“Cook!” said the Dwarfs,
 rubbing their tummies. “Hooray!
 She stays!” they cheered.
