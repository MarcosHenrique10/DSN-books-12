At that moment, the Queen was
 spying on Snow White and the Prince.
 When she saw them together, she flew
 into a jealous rage and rushed to her
 Magic Mirror, demanding an answer.

“Famed is thy beauty, Majesty, but
 hold! A lovely maid I see,” said the
 mirror. “Rags cannot hide her gentle
 grace. She is more fair than thee.”

“Reveal her name,” demanded the
 Queen.

“Lips red as a rose, hair black as
 ebony, skin white as snow . . .” the
 mirror told her.

“SNOW WHITE!” gasped the
 Queen.

Furious, she sent for her huntsman.
 “Take Snow White far into the forest,”
 the Queen instructed. “And there, my
 faithful huntsman, you will kill her!”

“But, Your Majesty,” the Huntsman
 protested, “the little princess!”

“Silence!” she said. “You know the
 penalty if you fail.”
