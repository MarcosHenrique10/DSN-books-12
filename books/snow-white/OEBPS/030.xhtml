Though the evil Queen was
 gone forever, the princess was still
 locked in her spell. So beautiful
 was she, even in death, the
 Dwarfs could not find it in their
 hearts to bury her.

Doc brushed away a tear. “Let’s
 make her a coffin out of glass
 and gold,” he said sadly. “That
 way, we can still see her and keep
 constant watch by her side.”
