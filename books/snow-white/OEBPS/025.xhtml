At the mine, they pulled and tugged at the confused little
 men. “What ails these crazy critters?” growled Grumpy.
 “They ain’t actin’ this way for nothin’.”

Doc thought about it. “Maybe it’s—the Queen!” he cried
 out, finally understanding the animals’ message.

Grumpy raced toward the cottage on the back of a deer.
 “Snow White’s in danger! We’ve gotta save her!” he said.
