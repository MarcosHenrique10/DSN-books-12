Once upon a time, there lived a lovely little princess named
 Snow White. Her vain and wicked stepmother, the Queen,
 feared that one day Snow White’s beauty would surpass her
 own. So she dressed the little princess in rags and forced her to
 work as a servant in the castle.

Each day, the Queen consulted her Magic Mirror and asked,
 “Magic Mirror on the wall, who is the fairest one of all?”

As long as the mirror responded in the Queen’s favor, Snow
 White was safe.
