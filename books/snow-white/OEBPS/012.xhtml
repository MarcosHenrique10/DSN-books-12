With the help of her animal friends, Snow White cleaned
 the cottage in no time. Then she decided to check upstairs.

“What adorable little beds!” she said, peering around the
 room. “And look—they have names carved on them. Doc,
 Happy, Sneezy, Dopey . . . and there’s Grumpy, Bashful, and
 Sleepy.” Snow White was feeling a little sleepy herself, so she
 took a nap across three of the tiny beds.
