Suddenly, Princess Hildegard rushes in,
late as usual, holding her pet mink, Lulu.
“Wait,” she calls.

“Please fill out this form, noting

your pet’s special talent,”

Baileywick says.

“Lulu does gymnastics, like
somersaults,” Hildy says.

“But because she’s so small,
she gets really nervous around
crowds. . . . Whoops!”

“Oh, no! Lulu, come back!”
cries Hildy as she chases
after her pet.
