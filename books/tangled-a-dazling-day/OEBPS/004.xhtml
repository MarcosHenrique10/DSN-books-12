Long ago and far away, there
lived a beautiful young woman
named Rapunzel. She had a
special gift: seventy feet of magical
hair. She had never, ever been
outside the tower where Mother
Gothel kept her hidden away.

Every year on her birthday, Rapunzel
gazed out her tower window at the sparkling
lights that rose into the nighttime sky. The
lights were meant for her— she was certain of it.
And Rapunzel yearned to leave the tower, just
once, to see those sparkling lights. . . .
