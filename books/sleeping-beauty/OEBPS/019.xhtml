Maleficent’s raven, perched on the chimney of the
cottage, had heard everything. It flew off to warn Maleficent that
the princess was finally returning.

Maleficent sped to the castle. There, using her evil
powers, she lured Aurora to a high tower. In the tiny room,
a spinning wheel suddenly appeared.

“Touch the spindle!” hissed Maleficent. “Touch it, I say!”
