Finally, Flynn decided they’d travel faster if they just started walking.

“Do you have this problem with all horses?” Rapunzel asked.

“No,” Flynn replied. “When I was growing up in the orphanage,
 I dreamed of becoming a swashbuckling hero—and swashbuckling
 heroes love horses. No, it’s this horse. He’s the problem!”

As they walked, Maximus flicked his tail in Flynn’s face. So Flynn
 poked Maximus in the ribs. Then Maximus stepped on Flynn’s foot . . .
 and Flynn yanked on Maximus’s bridle.
