Afterward, they could have ridden back to the kingdom, but
Flynn wouldn’t allow it. Instead, they walked!

“He ran all night,” Flynn explained, as he patted Maximus.
 “He’s tired and needs a rest.” Flynn paused. “You know, I felt like
 a real hero today.” Then he turned to Max. “But you are an even
 bigger hero. If it weren’t for you, Rapunzel and I wouldn’t be here.”

Flynn and Max were both heroes. But just as important, they
 were friends.
