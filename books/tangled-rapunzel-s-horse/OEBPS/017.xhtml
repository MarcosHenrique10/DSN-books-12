That evening, when it was time to see
 the lanterns being released, Flynn surprised
 Rapunzel by taking her out in a boat. Flynn said
 that watching from the water would give them the
 best view.

There was another surprise, too. Flynn gave a bag
 of apples to Maximus! The horse looked suspicious.

“What? I bought them,” Flynn responded.

Maximus whinnied happily as Flynn paddled away.

“Most of them,” Flynn muttered under his breath.
