Rapunzel and Flynn didn’t expect to be gone long. Maximus
 waited and waited.

Finally, a boat bumped up against the dock. But it was a
 different boat, and it was carrying only Flynn—who was tied to
 the mast with a stolen crown on his lap! He seemed confused, and
 was yelling, “Rapunzel? Rapunzel!”

Maximus backed out of sight as the palace guards came forward
 and arrested Flynn. Maximus didn’t know where Rapunzel was,
 but he knew that something had gone very, very wrong.
