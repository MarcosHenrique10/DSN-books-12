The day started nicely enough. It was the morning of Rapunzel’s
 eighteenth birthday, and the birds were singing. Just yesterday, she’d
 taken a huge step—she had ventured out from the tall tower that
 had been her home all her life! And soon, if all went well, she’d be in the
 kingdom for the first time, watching the beautiful floating lights that were
 released there every year on the same date . . . her birthday. It should
 have been a wonderful morning. But instead, here she was trying to
 rescue her guide from a horse!
