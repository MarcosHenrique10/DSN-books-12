Mulan helps Sofia climb onto a statue.
Sofia leaps to the next statue and the one after that.
Amber and Jun see they can do it, too,
and before too long . . .

“We made it!” Amber cries.

“See?” Mulan says.
“You girls are stronger than you thought.”

“But we still have to save our families,”
Sofia reminds them. “Can you help us, Mulan?”
