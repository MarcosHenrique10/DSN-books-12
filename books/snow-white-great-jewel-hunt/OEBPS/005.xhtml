“Farewell, my love!” said Snow White. The Prince was leaving on a royal trip. It was the first time the newlyweds would be apart.

“I’ll be home soon,” replied the Prince. “Until then, I’ve left an envelope for you on the well. It’s the first clue in a treasure hunt. At the end, you’ll find a special gift!”
