“Sofia,” her dad says, “I’m so proud of you.
You dance wonderfully!”

“Thank you, Your Majesty—I mean, Dad.”
“You know, Sofia, being a princess is about
having a good heart. And you’re going to make
a great princess.”

“Dad, I’ve been wondering . . .
why do they call you King Roland the Second?” she asks.

“Because my father was also named Roland,” he tells her.
