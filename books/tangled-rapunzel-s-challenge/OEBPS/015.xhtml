Flynn pointed to the river. “Let’s see who can cross this
 first,” he said. He jumped into the water.

Rapunzel didn’t know how to swim! How could she get
 across without swimming? Or using her hair?

“Any ideas?” she asked Pascal, who was hanging from a
 vine. The little chameleon shrugged. Then Rapunzel’s eyes lit
 up. “That’s it!” she cried.
