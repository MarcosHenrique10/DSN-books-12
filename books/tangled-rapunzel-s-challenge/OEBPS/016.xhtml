When Flynn climbed
 out of the river, Rapunzel
 was standing on the shore.
 “You got here first?” Flynn
 sputtered. “And you’re not
 even wet?”

Rapunzel nodded. “I
 swung across.”

Rapunzel held up a
 strong vine, that stretched
 back to a tall tree on the
 opposite side of the river.
 “I didn’t use my hair,” she
 explained. “I used this!”
