I can’t lose! Flynn thought. He doubled his speed . . .
 and tripped over Rapunzel’s hair! Before he knew it, he was
 completely tangled in her long locks.

Rapunzel kept running to the river’s edge. She won!

“That wasn’t fair,” Flynn panted. “You used your hair
 again.”

“No, I didn’t,” Rapunzel argued. “I only used my legs.”
 But Flynn insisted on one more contest.
