Rapunzel skipped happily along as
 Flynn Rider guided her through the forest.
 She had finally left her tower and was
 traveling to the kingdom!

“You know, Blondie,” Flynn said, “the
 forest can be dangerous. It’s no place for
 someone like you.”

“What do you mean, ‘someone like
 me’?” Rapunzel asked. “Anything you can
 do, I can do, too.”
