Flora, Fauna, and Merryweather, the three good fairies,
 also visited the castle to meet the new princess.

“Each of us the child may bless with a single gift,” Flora
 told the royal parents. As she stepped toward Aurora’s crib,
 she waved her wand and said, “Little princess, my gift shall
 be the gift of beauty.”

Fauna was next. “Tiny princess, my gift shall be the gift
 of song.”
