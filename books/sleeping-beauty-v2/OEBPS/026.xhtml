The fairies used their magic to put all the members of
 the kingdom in a deep sleep.

As Flora flew over King Hubert, he mumbled something
 about Prince Phillip. “Seems he’s fallen in love with some
 peasant girl,” he said sleepily.

Flora couldn’t believe it! “Peasant girl? Rose . . . Prince
 Phillip!” The good fairy realized that Aurora’s true love
 from the forest was actually her betrothed. He could
 break the curse!

“We’ve got to get back to the cottage!” Flora
 called out to the other fairies.
