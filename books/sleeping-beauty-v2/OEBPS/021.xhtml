The fairies finally revealed her royal identity.
 “Tonight we’re taking you back to your father,
King Stefan,” Flora said.

“But I can’t!” Aurora said sadly, thinking of her true
 love. “He’s coming here tonight.” Heartbroken, she
 walked to the castle with the fairies. Neither Aurora
 nor the fairies knew that Maleficent’s raven had
 overheard everything. He flew straight to Maleficent
 and told her the news. The sun hadn’t set
 on Aurora’s sixteenth birthday, so there
 was still time to fulfill the curse.
