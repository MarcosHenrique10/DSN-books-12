“A real birthday party!” Merryweather said.

“And a dress a princess can be proud of,” added Flora.

But even after sixteen years without magic, the fairies
 still had some trouble doing things the ordinary way. Flora
 and Merryweather struggled to make a dress. Fauna wasn’t
 quite sure how to fold eggs into cake batter.

Finally, the fairies decided to use their magic wands
 to make sure everything was perfect. But high in the sky,
 Maleficent’s wicked raven spotted the bursts of magic.

He had finally found where Aurora was hiding.
