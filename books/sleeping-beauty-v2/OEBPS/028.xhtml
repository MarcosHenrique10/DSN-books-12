Meanwhile, Prince Phillip arrived at the cottage, looking
 for the beautiful girl he’d danced with in the forest. The
 moment Phillip stepped inside, Maleficent’s henchmen
 attacked, and then they locked him in a dungeon in a
 faraway castle. Maleficent couldn’t risk Phillip’s breaking
 the curse with True Love’s Kiss.

Soon after, the three good fairies arrived—and Phillip
 was nowhere to be seen.
