At the castle, the fairies gave Aurora some time alone
 before she was presented as the princess. Then, suddenly,
 Maleficent appeared and placed Aurora in a powerful
 trance. Aurora unknowingly made her way down a secret
 passageway until she was in front of a spinning wheel!

“Touch the spindle. Touch it, I say!” Maleficent demanded.

Aurora obeyed and collapsed on the floor.
