While she was dancing with her animal friends, a stranger
 spotted her. It was Prince Phillip! He had overheard her
 conversation with the animals and asked her to dance, but
 she hesitated. She wasn’t supposed to talk to strangers.

“But don’t you remember?” he asked with a smile. “We’ve
 met before. You said so yourself: once upon a dream.”

They shared a dance through the forest and quickly fell in
 love, neither knowing the other’s name. They agreed to meet
 again at the cottage that evening.
