Briar Rose hurried home to tell her aunts about her true
 love! When she arrived, she saw their lovely surprise. “This
 is the happiest day of my life,” she said. “Just wait till you
 meet him.”

“Him?” Fauna said, worried.

“Oh, no!” gasped Merryweather.

“You’re already betrothed. To Prince Phillip, dear,”
 Fauna told her.
