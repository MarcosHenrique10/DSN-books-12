At last, Emile reached the end of the sewer
pipe that led to the rat colony. He poked his head
out and saw with relief that he hadn’t missed the
party after all! Hooray! he thought.

But when Emile tried to join the fun, he found
his belly was too big to fit through the end of the
pipe. “Help!” he cried. “Help! I’m stuck! I can’t get
out!”

Remy looked over at his brother and frowned.
He shook his head, annoyed. “I knew it!” he said.
“You’re stuck because you’re so full. You’re the
one who ate all my food last night!”
